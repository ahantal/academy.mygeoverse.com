Discover Track Curriculum

Four one-hour sessions · Four hours total · No prerequisites

01
SESSION 01

Spatial Intelligence & Open Standards

Explore how generative AI, spatial intelligence, and AI-enabled glasses are shaping the Spatial Web. Learn how open standards let devices, services, and applications work together across platforms.

02
SESSION 02

Visual Positioning & Site Scanning

VPS technology pinpoints a device's exact GeoPose, meaning its position and orientation. Multiset VPS is used to scan a site so it can support persistent, shared AR.

03
SESSION 03

Hands-on Spatial Content Placement

Use MyGeoVerse to place digital content at the mapped site. Experience how multiple users can view the same content in the same physical location across visits.

04
SESSION 04

Preparing Spatial Assets & Q&A

Prepare and upload GLB models and Unity packages for use in MyGeoVerse Smart Assets and proximity-based experiences. Close with an open Q&A.
