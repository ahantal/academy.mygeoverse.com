MyGeoVerse Academy

Learn. Create. Explore. Build.

Hands-on learning for the next generation of Spatial AI, Spatial Computing and Spatial Web creators.

MyGeoVerse Academy introduces students, developers, educators and technology communities to the technologies connecting AI with the physical world, then gives participants the opportunity to work with them directly.

Explore the Academy Bring Academy to Your Organization
Why MyGeoVerse Academy

A New Computing Paradigm Needs New Creators.

AI is beginning to move beyond screens and into the physical world.

Spatial Computing, geospatial technologies, AI-enabled devices and new forms of human-computer interaction are creating new possibilities for how digital information can understand and interact with real places.

MyGeoVerse Academy helps students, creators and technology communities understand this shift and begin building for it.

Spatial Intelligence

Understand how AI can gain context about physical places and environments.

Spatial Computing

Explore the technologies connecting digital experiences with the physical world.

Geolocation & Localization

Learn how GPS, Visual Positioning Systems and other localization technologies connect digital content with real-world locations.

Open Interoperability

Understand why open standards matter as spatial technologies evolve across devices, platforms and providers.

Current Program

From Awareness to Hands-On Creation.

The current MyGeoVerse Academy introductory program combines technology awareness with practical experience using the MyGeoVerse platform.

Current Introductory Program

2Sessions
2Hours Each
4Hours Total
Online / Instructor-Led
Hands-On
No Prior Spatial Computing Experience Required

This is the first Academy training format. The Academy itself is broader: learn, create, experiment, build, test and contribute.

01
SESSION 01

Spatial Intelligence Awareness

Understand the technological shift taking place as AI, Spatial Computing and the physical world begin to converge.

  • Spatial Web

    Introduction to the Spatial Web and the evolution from screen-based digital experiences toward spatially aware computing.

  • Generative AI + Spatial Intelligence

    Explore how Generative AI changes when digital intelligence begins to understand physical location and spatial context.

  • AI Glasses & Emerging Interfaces

    Explore the rise of audio-first AI, AI glasses and emerging interfaces beyond the smartphone.

  • Localization Technologies

    Understand the role of GPS, Visual Positioning Systems and other localization technologies in connecting digital experiences with real-world places.

  • Open Interoperability Standards

    Introduction to the role of open standards and interoperability in building a Spatial Web that can work across platforms, devices and providers.

02
SESSION 02

Hands-On Spatial Content Placement

Move from understanding Spatial Computing to creating and placing persistent digital content in the physical world using MyGeoVerse.

  • Creating a Physical Location for the Spatial Web

    Understand how physical locations become part of a geospatial content environment.

  • Uploading Assets

    Work with content including:

    GLB / 3D models2D imagesAudio filesVideo filesUnity Packages

  • Creating Assets

    Learn the basics of creating assets in Unity and customizing them through MyGeoVerse Studio.

  • Proximity-Based Assets

    Use the Proximity Based Asset Template to create a spatial experience activated by a visitor's relationship to a physical location.

  • Smart Assets If time permits

    If time permits, create a Smart Asset and explore how intelligent spatial content can interact with information associated with Points of Interest.

  • Content Placement

    Each participant deploys a geolocated AR object as part of the guided hands-on exercise.

Open Q&A and discussion of next steps.

Building for an Open Spatial Future

Learn More Than One Platform.

Spatial Computing will involve many devices, localization technologies, AI systems and spatial interfaces.

MyGeoVerse Academy introduces participants to the importance of interoperability and open standards alongside hands-on platform experience.
